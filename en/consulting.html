FAQ

Common questions.

Which package fits me?

One specific question: 30 minutes. Your planning is done and you want it checked: 60 minutes. You are just starting out: 90 minutes. And if you do not want to be on your own out there: the back office.

What if the time is not enough?

Then you simply book again. If we see that your subject is bigger than your package, we tell you during the call, not afterwards.

Can I move an appointment?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours beforehand. Just write to us by WhatsApp or email.

When does the back office start?

With your departure. The preparation beforehand is covered by the included video call. That way you get both at the right moment.

Is the back office an emergency service?

No. We are your organisation in the background: knowledge, contacts, judgement. Not a 24/7 emergency or breakdown service. You get an answer within 24 hours, usually much sooner.

Which route does the consulting cover?

Our focus is the west route from Europe to Cape Town. For other routes and regions, ask us briefly by WhatsApp first whether we can really help you. If we cannot, we will say so.

Still not sure which package fits?

Write and tell us what you are planning. We will tell you honestly what you need and what you do not.
