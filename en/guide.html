FAQ

Common questions.

What format does the guide come in?

A PDF, ready to download the moment you buy. You can read it on a phone, a tablet or a laptop, and offline on the road, which is where you will need it.

Which route does it cover?

The west route from Europe to Cape Town, currently the only realistic Trans Africa route. We explain why in the guide.

How current is the information?

The guide is based on our own drive from December 2025 to April 2026. It does not get fresher than that.

Can I return the PDF?

Once a digital file has been downloaded there is normally no right of withdrawal. We give you a guarantee anyway: if the guide does not help you, write to us within 30 days of buying and you get your money back.

Is the guide written for German travellers?

We are German and we travelled on German papers, so the visa table records what we paid and where we applied. The requirements differ by nationality. Everything else, the borders, the route, the vehicle, the costs and the daily life, applies whatever passport you carry.
